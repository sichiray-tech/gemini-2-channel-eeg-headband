#### What is it?
The **Gemini Dual-Channel EEG Headband** is an open, hackable brain-computer interface (BCI) wearable designed for makers, neurotech researchers, and creative technologists. Equipped with medical-grade sintered Ag/AgCl dry contact electrodes, it captures high-fidelity biopotential micro-signals directly from the frontal lobe without the mess of conductive gels or sticky pastes.

Gemini streams raw dual-channel EEG microvolt data as well as processed frequency bands (Delta, Theta, Alpha, Beta, Gamma) in real time. Backed by open data protocols and full API/SDK support (Python, C/C++, Unity, and serial stream parsers), it effortlessly bridges the gap between biological brainwave signals and interactive hardware, game engines, or cognitive research software.

#### Why did you make it?
Most clinical and commercial EEG systems on the market fall into two extremes: either multi-thousand-dollar medical rigs that demand messy saline gels and cumbersome setups, or closed-ecosystem consumer headbands that lock your raw data behind proprietary paywalls and obfuscated algorithms.

We built Gemini to give makers, developers, and researchers complete ownership of their neural data. We wanted a lightweight, comfortable wearable that can be thrown on in under ten seconds, connects seamlessly, and outputs raw, uncompressed, microvolt-level EEG directly to your favorite creative coding environments like Unity, Python, or microcontrollers.

#### What makes it special?
* **Precision Ag/AgCl Dry Electrodes:** Sintered Silver/Silver Chloride dry electrodes provide exceptional signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) and rapid stabilization without requiring messy conductive paste or prep wipes.
* **Dual-Channel Frontal Acquisition:** Configured specifically for frontal lobe sensing (ideal for Frontal Alpha Asymmetry, cognitive load estimation, focus tracking, and meditation state monitoring).
* **Reference Ear-Clip Integration:** Comes with a low-impedance grounding/reference ear clip to cancel common-mode noise and body motion artifacts.
* **Ergonomic & Breathable Design:** Flexible elastic strap with micro-adjustable tension ensures solid skin contact while remaining comfortable for prolonged tracking sessions.
* **Completely Open SDK & Raw Data Access:** Access raw microvolt (μV) waveforms, power spectral density (PSD), FFT band powers, and signal quality metrics without vendor lock-in.
* **Versatile BCI & HCI Applications:** Plug-and-play integration for neurofeedback training, attention/fatigue monitoring, bio-art installations, and hands-free assistive control.

### Technical Specifications
* **Channels:** 2-Channel Frontal Differential EEG + Reference / Ground
* **Electrode Type:** Sintered Ag/AgCl dry contact sensors
* **Signal Resolution:** 24-bit high-precision biopotential acquisition
* **Output Data:** Raw EEG waveform (μV), Frequency bands (Delta, Theta, Alpha, Beta, Gamma), Signal quality / Lead-off detection
* **Connectivity:** Low-latency Bluetooth / USB Serial communication
* **Power Supply:** Built-in rechargeable Lithium battery (Type-C charging)
* **Operating Battery Life:** Up to 6–8 hours of continuous data streaming
* **SDK Compatibility:** Python, Unity, C/C++, Arduino/ESP32 parsers, Web Bluetooth
